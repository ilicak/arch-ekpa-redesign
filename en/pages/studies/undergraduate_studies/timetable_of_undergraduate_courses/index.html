  • REGISTRATION FOR SEMINAR SI 95 WINTER SEMESTER 2026-2027. Seminar SI 95 will be taught this year in the winter semester every Thursday 9:00-12:00 (room 538). Those interested can send an email with their details to mpor@hotmail.gr (from your university account).   • II 114 – INTRODUCTION TO DIGITAL HISTORY.   • II 154 – DIGITAL HISTORY AND SPACE. Students are informed that the course II 154 Digital History and Space (7th Semester elective) will be held every Thursday between 12:00-15:00 in room 612.   • COURSE IA146 – BYZANTINE ARCHAEOLOGY. Students are informed that, due to an oversight, the wrong time and room were listed in the course schedule for the course IA146 – Byzantine Archaeology by Prof. Pl. Petridis. The course will be held every Wednesday, 12:00–15:00, in room 433. Classes will begin on Wednesday, October 7.
  • ANNOUNCEMENT OF PARTICIPATION IN SEMINAR SI 71 WINTER SEMESTER 2026-2027. Seminar SI 71 will be taught this year in the winter semester. Interested students should keep the following in mind: Engaging with epigraphic and literary sources requires an understanding of Ancient Greek, while knowledge of English for the bibliography is also necessary. See the course website https://eclass.uoa.gr/courses/ARCH706/ for the posted material for a more complete briefing on the topics. If, after informing yourself, you wish to participate, send an email directly to the address giannakn@arch.uoa.gr (from your university account) and not via e-class. Priority order will be strictly observed (fourth-year and senior/graduating students take precedence).

  • IA107 Excavation Conservation for Archaeologists_ Start of lectures. The elective (non-seminar) course (e-Class: ARCH1185): "IA107: Excavation Conservation for Archaeologists" will be held every Thursday 12:00-15:00 in Room 421. Start of winter semester classes: Thursday, October 8, 2026.

    The following will be examined:

    • The importance of conservation for the excavation, study, publication, preservation, and restoration of archaeological sites and findings.
    • "First aid" required during excavation for the safe detachment of objects made from sensitive materials or with a high degree of deterioration.
    • Technology of the manufacturing materials of the objects.
    • The packaging and transport of archaeological findings as well as the conditions for their safe storage.

    The following will be presented:

    • Techniques applied in the laboratory for the conservation of archaeological findings made from different raw materials.

    In parallel:

    • The teaching will be enriched with voluntary laboratory exercises in the Conservation Unit, for those who wish. Also, an effort will be made to organize visits to excavations and/or to sites where conservation or other related work is being carried out. The course is listed in the timetable with its description as "Basic Principles of Excavation Conservation".

    Michel Roggenbucke, mroggenbucke@arch.uoa.gr Dr. of Archaeology (Iron Archaeometallurgy), Laboratory Teaching Staff (E.DI.P.), Antiquities Conservator, MSc in Museum Studies.

    Student Reception (Office Hours): Tuesday and Thursday 10:00-12:00, Museum of Archaeology and History of Art (2nd floor), Room: "Conservation Unit" For more information, call: 210 727 7948
