DEPARTMENT OF HISTORY AND ARCHAEOLOGY

PEDAGOGICAL SUFFICENCY

Official content · Retrieved 22 October 2026. View current official source ↗

The degree of the Department of History and Archaeology of the NKUA secures the Pedagogical and Teaching Sufficiency to its graduates in order to allow their professional absorbsion in Secondary Education.

Students of the Department who started studying before 2013-2014 and its graduates are NOT subject to the regulations concerning the obligation for accredited Pedagogical and Teaching Sufficiency and the Degree of the Department of History and Archaeology of the School of Philosophy of the NKUA automatically secures their right to professional occupation in Secondary Education as teachers (PE02 Philologists), as had been in effect before Law 3848/2010.

For those who started studying during the academic year 2013-2014 up to 2018-2019, and in order to secure the Pedagogical and Teaching Sufficiency it is recommended to attend three Educational courses (additionally to the two compulsory base courses) as free elective courses. The two of the three recommended courses have to be ΤΕΚ300 "Educational Psychology" and ΤΕΚ126 "Curriculum. Teaching and Knowledge", offered by the Department of Educational Studies to the students of our Department, in the 5th and 6th semester of our indicative timetable. Students may elect the third of the recommended courses from all Educational courses offered by the Department of Educational Studies.

For those who started studying during the academic year 2019-2020 and later, the Pedagogical and Teaching Sufficiency is secured with their successful examination in the following courses:

  • ΙΙ04 Introduction to Historical Studies
  • Two seminars
  • The two Educational base courses: 
    • ΤΕΚ 301 (Pedagogy Ι) Histry Teaching, Theory and Practice,
    • ΤΕΚ 303 (Pedagogy ΙΙ), Theory and Practice of teaching philological courses. 
  • One of the following six (6) courses: 
    • ΙΑ 190 Museum pedagogy
    • ΙΙ126 History of Education
    • ΤΕΚ008 Organization and Administration of Education (taught in the winter semester)
    • ΤΕΚ004 sociology of Education
    • ΤΕΚ 300 (Pedagogy ΙΙΙ) Educational Psychology (taught in the winter semester)
