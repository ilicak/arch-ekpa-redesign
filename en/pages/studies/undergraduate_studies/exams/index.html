The Departmental Assembly (conv. No 9/27-4-2017) diecide to grant the possibility to the students of the 7th and 8th semster, as well as to the graduating students to ask an improvement of their grades in up to three (3) courses (compulsory and specialization electives). The submission deadline dates for applications for grade improvement  are the following: 30/10 until 10/11 and 20/3 until 30/3 every acad. year. It is noted that, with the submission of an application, the previous succesful grade, whose improvement the student applies, is deleted automatically. In addtion, the re-examination of the same course for a second time is not allowed. 

It is noted that students coming to the Department within the framework of the Erasmus programme have the option of either being examined or submit an essay in English.
