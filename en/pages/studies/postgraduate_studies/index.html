DEPARTMENT OF HISTORY AND ARCHAEOLOGY

POSTGRADUATE STUDIES

Official content · Retrieved 22 October 2026. View current official source ↗

The Department of History and Archeology was the first to establish a Postgraduate Program of Studies. This program officially operated from 1994 to 2021 and had nine directions. Currently, there are four departmental (PMS), two inter-departmental (DPMS) and two inter-institutional (DPMS) postgraduate programs. Teaching is in Greek or in English.

 

  • Archaeology and History of the Ancient World: from Prehistory to Late Antiquity

  • The Byzantine World: History and Archaeology

  • Modern and Contemporary History and History of Art

  • MA in Greek and Eastern Mediterranean Archaeology

  • Management of Monuments: Archaeology, City and Architecture

  • Museum Studies

  • MSc in Archaeological Science

  • MSc in Digital Humanities

  • European postgraduate study programs
