NKUA SCHOLARSHIPS

Official content · Retrieved 22 October 2026. View current official source ↗

Every year, the NKUA grants scholarships for undergraduate and postgraduate studies at home or abroad, as well as awards to students, authors of scientific treatises, etc. Scholarships and awards are granted, according to the will of the bequeather of each bequest, with certain conditions and, even, in several cases by competition, and in other cases by selection. The number of scholarships is not specific or the same every year, because it depends on the income of each endowment. In case of a competition, the courses in which the candidates are examined are determined based on the Indicative Curriculum of the Department.

The following list includes basic information on scholarships and endowment awards. For more information you may contact the Endowments Directorate - Scholarships and Awards Department (Chr. Lada 6, Athens).

  • SP. BALTATZI: Scholarships are given to people from Sirrako of Ioannina for Primary, Middle, High School and Higher Education. By selection.
  • MARIAS AVRASOGLOU: The grant supports a destitute student from the Schools of the NKUA, originally from Eastern Romilia. By selection.
  • DIMITR. ARAPOGLOU: Scholarships are given for the Philosophy, Medical, Dental and Theology Schools of all Greek universities to expatriates, preferably from Istanbul. By selection.
  • MATH. VARVARESOU: Scholarships are awarded to PhD holders or graduates with a grade of "very good" (at least) of the NKUA for postgraduate studies in Economics and Political Sciences in Europe and America. By competition.
  • IOANNOU VARYKA: Scholarships are given to destitute second-year students of the School of Philosophy (males only). By competition.
  • CON. GEROSTATHI: Scholarships are given to students of the NKUA from Arta. By competition.
  • I. DELLIOU or NAKIDOU: Scholarships are given for the improvement of young scientists in any science, except Medicine, in Europe, with preference to those originating from Eastern Macedonia.
  • E. THEODORIDOU-STIMSON: Scholarships are awarded for postgraduate studies at Universities in France and Russia to graduates of the Departments of the Philosophy School of the NKUA, based on their financial situation and the grade of the first year and by decision of the University Senate.
  • PETROU KANELLI: A scholarship is given to a student of the NKUA from Meligalas, Messinia. By selection.
  • KON/MIND CRISIS: Scholarships are awarded to graduates or PhD holders with a grade of (at least) "very good" from Greek universities, with preference to those from Hydra and Spetses, for postgraduate studies in Law, Political and Economic Sciences, Medicine and Dentistry, in Europe and America. By competition.
  • TRIAND. CRISIS: Scholarships are given to students of the Theoretical Schools of all Greek universities who come from Macedonia. By selection.
  • THEOD. MANOUSI: Scholarships are given to students of all universities in the country who come from Siatista. By selection. A scholarship is awarded to a Bachelor of Philology, a Bachelor of History and Archeology and a Bachelor of Theology for postgraduate studies in Western Europe, for three years. They must be former Scholars of the Endowment.
  • ANT. PAPADAKI: Scholarships are awarded to students of the NKUA. By competition.
  • S. PAPAZAFEROPOULOU: Scholarships are given to newly admitted students of all Greek universities who come from Andritsaina. By selection.
  • N. PAPAMICHALOPOULOU: Scholarships are given to students of the NKUA who come from Laconia and preferably from the village of Kremasti and the province of Epidaurus Limiras. By selection.
  • MARIAS RADOU: Scholarships are given to students of the School of Philosophy who come from Athens and Epirus. By competition.
  • SOFIAS SARIPOLOU: Scholarships are awarded to graduates or PhD holders from the School of Philosophy of the NKUA with a grade of (at least) "very good" or "excellent" in the field of their postgraduate studies or PhD programme in Europe and America. By competition.
  • MARIAS STAI: Scholarships are given to newly admitted university students who come from Kythira. By selection. Scholarships are awarded to university graduates, with a grade of (at least) "very good", who come from Kythera, for postgraduate studies in Europe and America. By selection.
  • I. SFOGOPOULOU: Scholarships are given to high school students who come from Portaria Volos. Scholarships are given to needy students of the NKUA who come from the 24 villages of Volos Magnesia. By competition.
  • GEORG. ARONI: The income of the trust is awarded to a Greek author, by decision of the Senate, who will have written not fiction, but a book of an educational, religious or other philosophical nature.
  • CON. KONTOU: (Konteio competition donation): A competition is announced every two years for the writing of a treatise with philological content.
  • PETROU KOSKOROZI: A competition is announced every five years for the writing of a treatise related to the sciences or the fine arts.
  • MARI-KAMARA POPE AWARD: Prizes are awarded every year to the best in performance and morale of the University's students.
  • PANTIA RALLI: Prizes are awarded every year to students of all Faculties of the NKUA, who stand out for their morals and progress, at the discretion of the Rector and the Senate.
  • NIKOLAOS TSAMBOULA AWARD: Awarded annually to a student of Medicine preferably, otherwise of Dentistry or another Faculty of the University of Athens, who comes from the Megalo Chorio of Evrytania or from Karpenisi or from the Mikro Chorio, Voutyros, Anada, Nostimos, Karitsa and Leather.
  • ΖΑΝΕΤ ΖΑΚΟΣ: The "Janet Zakos Charitable Foundation", based in Geneva, was established on 20 December 2005 for the purpose of granting scholarships to students of Byzantine Studies and/or the purchase and subsequent donation of Byzantine objects to museums that have wings with permanent Byzantine exhibitions. The scholarship is open to postgraduate students of the MSc "Byzantine World: History and Archaeology" and doctoral candidates in Byzantine History or Byzantine Archaeology of the Department of History and Archaeology of the University of Athens.

(Last update: 29/10/2025)
